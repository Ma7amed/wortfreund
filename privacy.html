Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Wortfreund has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your words, pictures and progress stay on your phone. A few features use online services, and this page lists each one, what it receives and when.

What stays on your phone

Your word groups, words, pictures, learning progress, review history and settings are stored in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. Android's own cloud backup is turned off for this app, so nothing is copied anywhere without you choosing it.

If you add a DeepL key, it is stored encrypted on your phone and is never included in any backup.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Wortfreund saves backups to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. The app can only see the files it created there, nothing else in your Drive. BrewedLabs never receives your backups.

  • What a backup contains: your words, groups, pictures (including photos you added), learning progress and review history, and your app settings. It does not contain your DeepL key, your Google account email or any device ID.
  • When it runs: when you tap "Back up now", automatically after you study while Drive is connected (this can use mobile data), and weekly if you turn that on (Wi-Fi only).
  • Your account email is read only to show which account is connected. It stays on your phone.
  • To stop and delete: disconnect Drive in Settings, then delete the app's data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

You can also save a backup file yourself. It has the same content and goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Online services

These services receive only what is needed for the feature. Like any website, they can see your IP address and may keep technical logs under their own privacy policies.

Online dictionary · kaikki.org

Receives
The word you search for, and sometimes its base form. Search starts while you type, so a partly typed word can be sent.
When
Only when the offline dictionary has no result and the online dictionary is on (Settings → Dictionary).

Picture search · BrewedLabs relay → Pixabay, Pexels

Receives
The search term, usually the word's English meaning, plus the picture type and safe search. The request goes through a small BrewedLabs relay on Cloudflare, so Pixabay and Pexels see Cloudflare's address, not yours. The relay stores no logs. It holds your IP address briefly in memory to limit abuse, and Cloudflare caches search results for up to 24 hours.
When
When you open the picture picker, and after you save a word without a picture if "Suggest a picture automatically" is on (Wi-Fi only).

Picture search · Openverse off by default

Receives
The search term, sent directly from your phone.
When
Only if you turn Openverse on as a picture source.

Picture downloads · image hosts

Receives
A normal image download. Pixabay, Pexels or, for Openverse, the site hosting the picture can see your IP address.
When
Previews load in the picture picker. The picture you choose is downloaded once and then kept on your phone.

Dictionary packs · GitHub

Receives
A normal file download, no personal data.
When
A weekly check for updated packs on Wi-Fi, and when you download the full dictionary.

DeepL translation off by default

Receives
The text to translate and your own API key.
When
Only if you turn DeepL on and add your key, and only when the dictionaries found nothing.

Voice · your phone's speech engine

Receives
The German word or phrase being spoken. Wortfreund prefers natural online voices when you're online. With Google's speech engine, that means the text is sent to Google to be spoken. Offline voices run on the phone.
When
Whenever a word is spoken.

Phone translator · Google ML Kit

Receives
A one-time download of the German–English language model. Translations then run on your phone. Google says ML Kit may send anonymous usage and diagnostic data.
When
After setup or from Settings, following your "Wi-Fi only" choice.

Updates · Google Play

Receives
Google Play's standard update check.
When
When you open the Today screen.

Feedback

"Send feedback" in Settings → About opens your own email app with our address filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and the app adds no logs or device details.

What Wortfreund doesn't do

  • No ads, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No device IDs or advertising IDs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state for the online features above, and to wait for Wi-Fi when you ask for that.
  • Notifications for the optional daily reminder.
  • Background work (foreground service, wake lock, start at boot) so downloads, backups and your reminder can finish reliably.

Wortfreund asks for no camera, storage, location or contacts permission. When you add your own picture, Android's photo picker or camera app hands over only the picture you choose.

Children

Wortfreund is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
