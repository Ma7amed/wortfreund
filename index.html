Your words

Every article in its own color.

Blue, red or green tells you der, die or das at a glance, so the article sticks without a grammar table. Every word has a photo, a clear voice and its plural.

der · masculinedie · femininedas · neuter

Start with ready-made A1 groups, or build your own for each textbook lesson. Want to test yourself? Hide the German and tap a word to peek.

Practice right away

Three short steps until a word is yours.

New words are practiced in turns, a few at a time. A word you miss shows up again a few questions later.

  1. 1Listen, then pick the meaning.
  2. 2Pick the right sound out of four.
  3. 3Say it yourself, then check.

Daily reviews

Words come back just before you'd forget them.

Wortfreund schedules reviews with FSRS, a modern spaced-repetition method. Words you know come back less often, hard ones sooner. Most days take a few minutes.

Pick how you're asked: listen and guess, check the article, pick the German word, or say it from a picture.

Dictionary

Search in English or German, even offline.

Everyday words come first, with gender, plural and meaning. Add any result to a group in one tap. For whole phrases, the phone's translator or your own DeepL key fills in.

A calm daily rhythm

A clear finish line every day.

Set how many new words a day suit you. When they're done, Wortfreund says so, and keeps your streak without nagging. Want more? Learn five extra any time.

Your progress can back up to your own Google Drive, so a new phone doesn't mean starting over.

Light and dark

Easy on the eyes, morning or night.

A quiet design in light and dark. Music and podcasts get quieter while a word is spoken, then come back.
